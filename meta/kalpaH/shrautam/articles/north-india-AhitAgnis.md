Indeed, this narrative that श्रौत rituals such as have died out in North is false and insulting to all those who dedicate their life to it's preservation, here's a thread on some great श्रौतिन् vidvāns of North and their deeds.

First I would mention my own dādāgurudeva, he is the writer of texts such as क्षौरदर्श, श्रौतोल्लास, अन्त्यकर्म श्रद्धाप्रकाश (of गीताप्रेस), संन्यासग्रहण पद्धति etc. and had srautādhāna in 1960. He was one of the closest grhastha aides of धर्मसम्राट स्वामी करपात्री जी महाराज.

You must have seen a familiar face in one image below, that's because the next vidvān is –>
श्री राघवाचार्य जी महाराज. Yes! स्वामीजी is a student of ब्रह्मचारी गुरुजी and has studied षडङ्ग वेद from him as a ब्रह्मचारिन् himself, he's a निष्णात विद्वान् of श्रुति and officiates at many rites.
Among ब्रह्मचारी गुरुजी's students, another great vidvān is अग्निहोत्री श्री अरविन्द पाण्डेय जी. He is a नित्याग्नीहोत्री and regularly performs rites such as मित्रविन्देष्टि for बटुकs to see and learn from at places such as Jñāna pravāha in काशी etc.
Other than ब्रह्मचारीजीs शिष्यवृन्द, many other great srauta scholars also flourish in the north, such as आहिताग्नि श्री ज्ञानेन्द्र सापकोटा जी from काशी, who is faculty at संपूर्णानन्द uni. He is also one of the associates of श्री मोहित भारद्वाज जी in reviving the शाङ्खायन शाखा.
and lastly but certainly not the least, our beloved आचार्य श्री मोहित भारद्वाज @vvaayu जी who is also an आहिताग्नि and is transmitting this vidyā to the many ब्रह्मचारिन्s of his gurukulas @vaidikabharata
May the efforts of such dharmātmans uplift dharma!
