##### Translation
11. When the lord of riches, angry, took thy milk, O cow, then this the  
firmament (*nā́ka*) now keeps (*rakṣ*) in three vessels.

##### Notes
Ppp. reads, in **a-b**, *-patiḥ kṣīraṁ dehi bharad vaśe*.

</details>
