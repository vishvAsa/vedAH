20. Agni sends heat from the sky; the wide atmosphere is god Agni's;  
mortals kindle Agni \[as\] oblation-bearer, ghee-lover.

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - ऋक् (यन्त्र-स्वरः)</summary>
