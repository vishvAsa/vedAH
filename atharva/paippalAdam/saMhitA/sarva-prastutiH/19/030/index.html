Translation
  1. Let Vāiśvānara (Agni) by his rays purify us, the wind, lively with

Notes

The verse is found also in TB. (i. 4. 8³) and MS. (iii. 11. 10). They
read mā for nas in a and d, mayobhū́s (which is decidedly

in c. Pāda c is jagatī.
