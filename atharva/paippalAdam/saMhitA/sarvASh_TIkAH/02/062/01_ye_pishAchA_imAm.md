<details><summary>विस्तारः (द्रष्टुं नोद्यम्)</summary>

वित्ति should be अन्तोदात्त here by "मन्त्रे वृषेषपचमनविदभूवीरा उदात्तः".  

I could find it only in "वि॒त्तं च॑ मे॒ वित्ति॑श्च मे", which indeed has the regular accent of the क्तिन्-प्रत्यय। I don't have any particular explanation other than that Vedic texts often contain some words here and there which are more typical of a later language.
</details>
