> What is your opinion on Śāṅkhāyana?  
The udgātṛ does upasthāna of the sañjñapta puruṣa (i.e. the man who has been "silenced" by suffocation) with the yāma-sāman, the hotṛ does so with with the puruṣasūkta, and then all four (hotṛ, brahman, adhvaryu, and udgātṛ) recite "pareyivā́ṃsaṃ praváto mahı̄́r ánu..." two by two.

Two possibilities - it was an old practice with one particular school is recommending and other schools simply ignoring OR it is just a proposal from one school based on a particular brahmavAda and the proposal never went beyond draft state and never ratified.  
I fail to see how only the first option is necessarily true. This is despite the availability of full details in the proposal.
