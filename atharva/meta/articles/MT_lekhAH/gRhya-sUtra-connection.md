> KYV Hiraṇyakeśi gṛhyasūtra (1.15.6) :
>
> rudra nīlaśikhaṇḍa vīra karmaṇi karmaṇi |
imaṃ me pratisaṃvādinaṃ vṛkṣam ivāśaninā jahi ll

Good catch. There is close mantra from AV-paippalAda for the same viniyoga:
sharva nIlashikhaNDa
vIra karmaNi-karmaNi |
imAm asya prAshaM jahi
yenedaM vivadAmahe ||

Unlike taittirIya mantra-s which are indicated by pratIka, hiraNyakeshin gives this in full. Hence, we have suspected this and a some other from this and the following section of the gR^ihyasUtra are from a lost AV tradition.
