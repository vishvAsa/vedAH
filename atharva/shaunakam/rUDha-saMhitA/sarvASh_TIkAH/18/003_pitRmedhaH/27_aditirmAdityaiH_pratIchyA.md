<details><summary>Whitney</summary>

### Translation
27. Let Aditi with the Ādityas protect me from the western quarter;  
arm-moved etc. etc.

### Notes
</details>

<details><summary>Griffith</summary>

From westward Aditi was Adityas save me! etc.
